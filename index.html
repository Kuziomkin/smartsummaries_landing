FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Smart Summaries.

play_arrow What does Smart Summaries actually do?

Smart Summaries helps you understand documents from photos. You take a picture of a document, and the app extracts the text, explains it in plain language, highlights key points, and can translate it to your language.

It is designed to reduce confusion, not to make decisions for you.

play_arrow Is this legal advice?

No.

Smart Summaries does not provide legal advice or recommendations. It explains what the text says and highlights what may need review so you can decide what to do next or consult a professional if needed.

play_arrow What types of documents work best?

Smart Summaries works well with:

  • Government and official letters
  • Contracts and agreements
  • Bills and invoices
  • Policies, forms, and notices
  • Scanned documents and screenshots

Very handwritten or low-quality images may produce weaker results.

play_arrow Does it support multiple languages?

Yes.

You can translate extracted text into 70+ commonly used languages and receive the summary in the same language.

play_arrow Are my documents stored or uploaded?

Your document history is saved locally on your device so you can revisit it later. Smart Summaries is built to minimize data retention and does not act as a document archive or cloud storage service.

play_arrow How accurate are the summaries?

The app is designed to improve understanding, not to be perfect.

Summaries reflect the content of the document but may miss nuance, formatting, or context. Always double-check important details in the original document.

play_arrow Can I use it for sensitive documents?

You can use Smart Summaries for personal documents, but you should avoid relying on it alone for critical legal, financial, or medical decisions. The app is an interpretation aid, not a replacement for professional review.

play_arrow How do pricing and cancellations work?

Smart Summaries offers one scan per day for any users and up to 50 scans for subscribed users.

Subscriptions are managed through the App Store and can be canceled at any time.

play_arrow What should I do if something doesn’t look right?

If a summary or highlight seems unclear or incorrect:

  • Review the original document
  • Try rescanning with a clearer photo
  • Contact support at support@smartsummaries.app

Smart Summaries is meant to assist, not replace your judgment.

play_arrow Can I request new features or improvements?

Yes.

Feedback from real users directly influences how Smart Summaries evolves. You can submit feature requests or suggestions through the app or support email.
